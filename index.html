Welcome to HAVOK Bike Park

HAVOK Bike Park is a purpose-built mountain biking destination nestled on the dramatic edge of the Calder Valley, spread across approximately 100 acres of steep, rolling hillside. Overlooking the awe-inspiring gorge of Cliviger, the park offers incredible panoramic views and a riding experience that’s as scenic as it is fun. Designed for riders of all abilities, HAVOK features a diverse range of trails — from flowing beginner-friendly lines to technical descents that will challenge even the most experienced riders. Whether you're here to progress your skills or push your limits, there’s something for everyone. Our dedicated dirt jump zone sits in one of the park’s most stunning spots, giving riders the chance to catch big air while soaking in some of the most amazing views in the region.

Come ride, connect, and experience the energy of a bike park built by riders, for riders.

As a Community Interest Company (CIC), HAVOK exists for the benefit of riders and the wider community. We're more than just a bike park — we're a hub for connection, progression, and shared passion. We’re committed to building a welcoming, inclusive, and safe space for riders of all backgrounds and abilities.

At the heart of everything we do is community, craftsmanship, and care — not only for our riders but also for the land we ride on. We are dedicated to preserving the environment and respecting the wildlife that shares our space. Sustainability and stewardship guide the way we build and maintain trails, and shape the future of HAVOK.

New features, trails, and improvements are being added all the time, so stay connected with us on Facebook and Instagram for the latest updates.

We’re proud to be rider owned and operated, and we can’t wait to welcome you to HAVOK.

Find us

We are located in Todmorden in the north of the UK. To get to us, turn onto Station Parade and follow the road to the railway crossing. You must exit your vehicle and press the button on the barriers to lift them.


!!! YOU MUST CLOSE THE BARRIER BEHIND YOU !!!


Parking is available on site; take a left just after the barriers and a second immediate left!
