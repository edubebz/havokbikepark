PARK RULES

These rules are in place for everyone's safety, not just yours! They are NON-NEGOTIABLE, please respect them as we reserve the right to refuse entry to anyone not adhering to them.

Please check out our Disclaimer before arriving at the park, and fill out as approriate for all people intending the ride the park. This only needs completing once!



1️⃣ Full-face helmets and knee pads must be worn at all times while riding.
2️⃣ Riders under 12 must have parent/guardian supervision at all times while riding.
3️⃣ Riders under 14 must have a parent/guardian on-site while riding.
4️⃣ Riders under 16 must be signed in by a parent/guardian on their first visit.
5️⃣ All riders must read and sign the Havok Rider Disclaimer before using any trails or facilities.
⚠️ You ride entirely at your own risk.

Equipment and Conduct

1️⃣ Your bike must be in safe, working condition, with suitable components for the terrain – disc brakes and suspension are required.
2️⃣ Riding under the influence of alcohol or drugs is strictly prohibited.
3️⃣ Stay on designated trails – do not cut corners, modify features, or create unofficial lines.
4️⃣ Never ride back up the trails. Do not stop or linger on the track unless absolutely necessary (e.g., emergency or injury).
5️⃣ Ride with care and respect for others – including slower riders, walkers, and HAVOK staff.
6️⃣ Obey all trail signage and follow instructions related to maintenance, trail closures, or diversions.

Fire Road and Uplift Safety


1️⃣ Book on for your uplift pass and sign in at the Sign-In Hut when you arrive.
2️⃣ Wristbands must be worn on your wrist – not on your bike or bag.
3️⃣ Only wait in designated loading areas – do not block fire road or trail entrances/exits.
4️⃣ Full-face helmets must be worn and fastened at all times on the uplift vehicle.
5️⃣ Load your bike only after the driver has verified your wristband or token.

By entering and riding at HAVOK, you agree to follow these rules and ride responsibly. They’re here to keep you, others, and the trails safe.

Need help or have questions? Speak to a member of the HAVOK team on-site or email us at enquiries@havokmtb.co.uk.
