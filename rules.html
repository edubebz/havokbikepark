PARK RULES

These rules are in place for everyone's safety, not just yours! They are NON-NEGOTIABLE, please respect them as we reserve the right to refuse entry to anyone not adhering to them.

Please check out our Disclaimer before arriving at the park, and fill out as approriate for all people intending the ride the park. This only needs completing once!



1️⃣ Full-face helmets and knee pads must be worn at all times while riding.
2️⃣ Riders under 12 must have parent/guardian supervision at all times while riding.
3️⃣ Riders under 14 must have a parent/guardian on-site while riding.
4️⃣ Riders under 16 must be signed in by a parent/guardian on their first visit.
5️⃣ All riders must read and sign the Havok Rider Disclaimer before using any trails or facilities.
⚠️ You ride entirely at your own risk.

Equipment and Conduct

1️⃣ Your bike must be in safe, working condition, with suitable components for the terrain – disc brakes and suspension are required.
2️⃣ Riding under the influence of alcohol or drugs is strictly prohibited.
3️⃣ Stay on designated trails – do not cut corners, modify features, or create unofficial lines.
4️⃣ Never ride back up the trails. Do not stop or linger on the track unless absolutely necessary (e.g., emergency or injury).
5️⃣ Ride with care and respect for others – including slower riders, walkers, and HAVOK staff.
6️⃣ Obey all trail signage and follow instructions related to maintenance, trail closures, or diversions.

Fire Road and Uplift Safety


UPLIFT RULES Our uplift service is operated to safely and efficiently transport riders and their bikes to the top of the trails. All riders using the uplift must follow the instructions of the uplift driver and Havok Bike Park staff. Booking & Sign-In

•⁠ ⁠All riders must sign in and have a valid uplift pass before using the uplift.
•⁠ ⁠Uplift users will be briefed on the loading procedure when they sign in.
•⁠ ⁠If you are unfamiliar with the uplift, please ask the driver or a member of the Havok team for assistance before your first run.

Loading & Unloading Bikes

•⁠ ⁠Every rider must be physically capable of lifting, loading and securing their own bike onto the uplift trailer.
•⁠ ⁠The uplift driver will supervise the loading process and may demonstrate or assist with the first run where necessary.
•⁠ ⁠Riders are expected to load and unload their own bikes on subsequent runs. The driver cannot routinely load or unload bikes for riders throughout the day.
•⁠ ⁠Bikes must only be loaded or unloaded when instructed by the driver, and riders must follow all instructions regarding where and how their bike is positioned.
•⁠ ⁠Riders are responsible for ensuring their bike is correctly secured and must report any concerns to the driver immediately.
•⁠ ⁠Bikes must be suitable for transportation on the uplift and any loose items must be removed or securely fastened.

Younger Riders

•⁠ ⁠Younger riders may use the uplift where Havok considers it suitable for their age, ability and circumstances.
•⁠ ⁠A parent or guardian must be present and able to assist with loading and securing the bike if the younger rider cannot safely do this themselves.
•⁠ ⁠Depending on their age and circumstances, younger riders may be required to travel inside the uplift vehicle rather than independently in the trailer/cage.
•⁠ ⁠If suitable assistance is not available, the rider may not be able to use the uplift.

Uplift Safety

•⁠ ⁠Riders must remain in the designated area and follow the driver’s instructions at all times.
•⁠ ⁠Do not enter the loading area or approach the trailer until instructed to do so.
•⁠ ⁠Riders must not interfere with other bikes or attempt to move another rider’s bike.
•⁠ ⁠Seatbelts must be worn where provided and riders must not distract the driver while the vehicle is moving.
•⁠ ⁠The driver may refuse to load a bike or allow a rider to use the uplift if they consider the bike, rider or loading process to be unsafe.

Havok Bike Park reserves the right to suspend or stop the uplift where necessary due to weather, trail conditions, mechanical issues or any other safety concern.

By using the uplift, riders agree to follow these rules and the instructions of the uplift driver and Havok Bike Park staff. Failure to do so may result in removal from the uplift without refund.
