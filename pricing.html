Pricing & Memberships

Flexible options built for every rider.


Single uplift runs will remain at £5 per run (payment is made directly to the Uplift crew).
Single runs will primarily be available on wet or quieter days when uplift is not guaranteed to operate continuously throughout the day.
Please note that uplift pricing may vary for special events.

Please see our Park Rules page to ensure you are eligible to ride before you arrive!

Memberships

Membership remains a huge part of what makes HAVOK special. It helps us demonstrate the strength of our community, supports future funding opportunities, and allows us to continue investing back into the park for everyone to enjoy.

Membership Benefits Include:

  • 👉 Discounted uplift day passes
  • 👉 Merchandise discounts
  • 👉 Early access to event tickets
  • 👉 Exclusive midweek riding access
  • 👉 Member-only ride sessions and events
